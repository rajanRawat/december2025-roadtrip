Day Three

Royals & Divine Tales

Grandeur and the Shift to Spirituality

🏯 The Mighty Amer Fort

This morning, we explore the massive ramparts of Amer Fort. It's a real-life castle experience the kids will love! Following a guided tour and a visit to the rugged Jaigarh Fort, we enjoy a local lunch before beginning our journey toward the sacred twin cities of Mathura and Vrindavan.

Amer Fort

Explore grand courtyards, mirrored halls, and panoramic views. A UNESCO World Heritage Site!

Jaigarh Fort

Home to the world's largest cannon on wheels! Kids love the massive Jaivana cannon.

As we leave royal Rajasthan behind, we enter the sacred land of Lord Krishna. The spiritual energy of Mathura-Vrindavan awaits!

Day Four

The Land of Lights

Divine Experiences & Dazzling Finales

Krishna Janmabhoomi

Visit the sacred birthplace of Lord Krishna—feel the divine energy of this ancient site.

ISKCON Vrindavan

Experience the evening aarti and melodious bhajans at this beautiful temple.

Our final day immerses us in the vibrant spirituality of Lord Krishna's land. We visit the historic Krishna Janmabhoomi Temple in the morning. After a restful afternoon, we head to Vrindavan for evening darshan at ISKCON.

🌟 Prem Mandir Light Show — The Grand Finale

The grand finale of our trip is the mesmerizing light show at Prem Mandir— a visual treat that will leave the whole family spellbound! Watch as the white marble temple transforms into a rainbow of colors, synchronized to devotional music. It's pure magic!
